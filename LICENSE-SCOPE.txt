Pinball Port licensing scope

The newly written game implementation under src/rewrite, its tests under
tests/rewrite, the portable Creator/builder under tools/portable, and the
project launchers and build scripts are licensed under GNU GPL version 3
only (SPDX: GPL-3.0-only), selected by the project owner on 2026-11-17.
The full license text is in LICENSE.

Original XP game assets and cover art are excluded from this source and
Creator distribution. They retain separate rights and are not relicensed
by this grant. The Creator obtains or imports the required assets
separately for local game creation.

External SDK/compiler/runtime components retain their original licenses.
Their retained notices are under tools/portable/runtime-notices and are
copied into created game folders. The bundled archive reader has its
notice, source archive and provenance under payload/.

No original Pinball implementation is compiled into the rewritten game.
Source-derived behavior and data-format observations informed the new code;
this project does not claim a legally verified clean-room process.
